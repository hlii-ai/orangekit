# AI SEO Optimization Guide

A guide to optimizing content so it gets cited and extracted by AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and the like).

## The 3-pillar evaluation framework

### Pillar 1: Structure (40 points) - extractability

Evaluates the structural elements that let an AI parse the content and include it in an answer.

Extractable block types: definition blocks, step-by-step blocks, comparison tables, FAQ blocks, statistics blocks, list blocks.

### Pillar 2: Authority (35 points) - citability

Evaluates how likely an AI is to cite the content as a trustworthy source.

Improving factors: source citations (+40%), statistics/data (+37%), expert quotes (+30%), an authoritative tone (+25%), E-E-A-T signals (+20%).

### Pillar 3: Presence (25 points) - discoverability

Evaluates whether AI crawlers can reach and index the content.

Essentials: bots allowed in robots.txt, third-party presence, Schema.org structured data, sitemap/indexing.

## Platform-Specific Notes

- **Google AI Overviews**: Featured Snippet format, FAQ Schema extracted first
- **ChatGPT**: direct answers + source citation, OAI-SearchBot must be allowed
- **Perplexity**: source diversity + recency, PerplexityBot must be allowed
- **Gemini**: ties into the Google Knowledge Graph, Google-Extended must be allowed
- **Claude**: built on Brave Search, ClaudeBot / anthropic-ai must be allowed
